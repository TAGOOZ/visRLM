```
The core insight: RLMs aren't just about LLMs calling themselves recursively.

The deeper insight is LLMs *interacting with their own prompts as objects* in an external environment (Python REPL).

This enables:
• 100x context scaling
• 110%+ performance gains
• Cheaper inference costs
```
